About Me

My name is Jackson Brown, and I am a Pre-Business student at BYU, passionate about supply chain management and sustainability issues. I grew up in Alexandria, Virginia, right outside of Washington D.C., where I developed an early interest in how global systems impact local communities. Through hands-on experience in project management, information systems, and supply chain operations, I’ve gained a strong foundation in both the strategic and technical sides of business. I'm driven by a desire to create more efficient, ethical, and sustainable systems, and I’m excited to continue developing these skills as I pursue a career in supply chain management. I thrive in collaborative environments and enjoy working on cross-functional teams to solve complex problems. As I move forward in my studies, I look forward to finding innovative ways to align business goals with environmental and social responsibility.
